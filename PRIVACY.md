# Privacy Policy

**Open Currency Converter** (shown on the home screen as "Converter")
Effective date: 21 October 2026

Open Currency Converter is a currency converter with no ads, no accounts and no
tracking. This policy explains the little data the app touches.

## What we collect

Nothing. The developer does not collect, store, receive or sell any personal
data. The app has no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking
code, and no account or sign-in.

## What stays on your device

The app saves your currency list, favourites, own exchange rates, settings and
the most recently downloaded rates in the app's private storage on your
device, so it works offline. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling
the app, or clearing its data, deletes it.

## Network requests

To update exchange rates, the app downloads public rate files from
[jsDelivr](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net) or,
if that fails, from [Cloudflare Pages](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/),
which host the open [Exchange API](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/exchange-api)
data. These requests contain no personal information or identifiers, but like
any web request they reveal your device's IP address and basic request details
(such as the time) to those providers, under their privacy policies. The app
makes no other network requests.

**Buy me a coffee** in Settings opens the donation page in your browser. The
app itself sends nothing; anything you do on that page is covered by Ko-fi's
privacy policy.

## Clipboard and sharing

The app reads the clipboard only when you choose **Paste**, and writes to it
only when you choose **Copy**. When you choose **Share**, the text is passed to
the share sheet of your device, and you decide where it goes.

## Permissions

On Android the app requests only the Internet permission, to download rates.
It requests no other permissions on any platform.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including
children.
